# Review: Mufahras showcase, 16:9 (46.5 s)

Generated by `tools/build.py`; edit that and re-run it, never the compositions directly.

## Creative calls
- The voice is the clock: each scene starts on the first word of its line (`assets/vo/transcript.json`, voice at 0.3 s).
- Captions show what was said, not the original script ("It can search by meaning", no "six" before
  "hadith collections"). Scenes 7 and 8 have no captions; their words are the titles.
- Product UI is shown flat whenever it is read. The 3D wall in the browse scene is texture only; each named
  screen rises out of it and lands flat.
- Quranic and Arabic text is never animated per letter or broken into points: the points in the meaning
  scene come from the English query only.
- No music. Sound is a quiet wind bed plus effects on visible actions (keys, taps, slides, whooshes).
- Voice normalised to -16 LUFS, peak -1.6 dBFS (original kept at `video/demo-voiceover`).

## Measured vs looked at
- `hyperframes check`: no errors. Four `line_unread` warnings kept on purpose: the Arabic query and the
  quote dim or fade with the voice, the ghost queries are meant to fade out of reach, and the device tag
  holds 1.5 s under a 1.7 s floor before the zoom.
- Looked at: snapshots of every beat and seam. The sound was measured, not heard.

## Weakest shots, and what more time would change
- Hadith gradings: the rows lift out as screenshot strips; rebuilding the table as live rows with the badges
  popping separately would read better.
- Word scene is 3 s; the explanation panel barely lands before the cut.
- The open's lockup sits left of centre while the pattern collapses into the icon.
